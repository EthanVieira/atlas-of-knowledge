      ✧ Welcome to the Atlas of Knowledge

      A star map of human learning. Each star is a course; lines run from a subject to the courses that build on it. Foundations sit near the core, advanced work out at the rim, and each field is its own constellation.

      Getting around

      • Drag to pan, scroll to zoom
      • Click a star to open its course card
      • Click a prerequisite to trace its lineage
      • Use the minimap to leap across the atlas

      Find & focus

      • Search by course name or topic
      • Filter fields to declutter the map
      • Click a legend field to fly to it
      • Rings show depth: foundations → advanced

      Track your journey

      • Tick a card's ✓ to mark it complete (prerequisites first)
      • Set a 🎯 goal for an ordered study plan
      • Discover suggests what to take next — Smart follows your goal or leverage
      • Export / Import to back up or move progress
